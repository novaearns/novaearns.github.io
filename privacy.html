Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Who we are

Nova Earns publishes educational videos on YouTube and Instagram. Contact: novaearnsnow@gmail.com.

The studio-mcp application

studio-mcp is an internal tool used only by the owner of the Nova Earns channels. It uses YouTube API Services to upload videos, set their details and schedules, and read basic channel statistics on channels owned by the Nova Earns account. It is not offered to the public and does not access any other person's account.

Data we access and how we use it

  • OAuth tokens for the owner's own YouTube channels, stored locally on the owner's computer, used only to perform the actions above.
  • Channel and video information (titles, descriptions, statistics) of the owner's channels, used only to manage those channels.

We do not collect, sell or share personal data of viewers or third parties. No data is used for advertising.

Website

This website does not use cookies or analytics and does not collect personal data.

Google and YouTube

By using YouTube you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access granted to studio-mcp can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the tool is in use and can be deleted at any time by revoking access. For any request, write to novaearnsnow@gmail.com.
